The Story

More than a cup of coffee.

Élora began with an idea that felt almost old-fashioned — that a good cup deserves time.

We source single-origin beans from small estates, roast them in-house every morning, and let every drink settle before it reaches your table. Our kitchen follows the same rhythm. Croissants are laminated over three days. Produce is picked up before sunrise. Nothing leaves the counter unless it feels right.

The result is a menu that reads more like a quiet ritual than a rush — one we are happy to share with you, any day of the week.

The House Signature

The Experience

A room that changes with the light.

01

Morning — sunlight and filter coffee.

The shutters go up at eight. The first pour-over of the day is always Ethiopian, always brewed slowly. Tables by the window fill first, and the room stays quiet enough to hear the grinder working.

  • 08:00 — 12:00
  • Filter & Pour Over
  • Fresh Bakery
02

Afternoon — long tables, longer talks.

By two o'clock the room belongs to conversation. Plates are shared, laptops stay out, and the espresso bar keeps a steady rhythm. The terrace opens whenever the Delhi weather allows it.

  • 12:00 — 17:00
  • Kitchen Full Menu
  • Terrace Seating
03

Evening — low light, slow finish.

After sundown the music drops and the lights warm. Desserts come out of the kitchen, the affogato gets ordered at nearly every table, and nobody is rushed toward the door.

  • 17:00 — 23:00
  • Dessert Service
  • Reservations Advised
